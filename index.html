Frequently asked questions

What does the AI actually do?

It outpaints: the AI studies your photo's content, lighting and style, then generates new pixels around the edges to fill the larger canvas. Your original photo is never altered or cropped.

How do credits work?

1 credit = 1 extended image. You start with 1 free trial credit. When you run out, buy more with PayPal or card — $5 → 50 credits, $10 → 100 credits, $15 → 150 credits. You'll get a single-use code, paste it in, and the credits land in your secure wallet on our server. No account, no subscription, no card stored anywhere.

Do my photos get stored?

No. Your image is sent to the AI provider for the few seconds of processing, then discarded. Nothing is kept on our servers.

What sizes work best?

Modest expansions (e.g. 4:3 → 16:9) look the most natural. Extreme flips (square → tall 9:16) work but give the AI a lot of canvas to invent — results vary.

Is there a subscription?

No — JSM Extend is strictly pay-per-use. Buy credits when you need them, use them whenever. Nothing renews, nothing is charged automatically.

Do credits expire?

Never. Your balance stays in your wallet until you spend it, whether that's tomorrow or next year.

What if the AI fails to generate?

You keep your credit. Failed generations are refunded automatically — you only ever pay for images you actually receive.

Why don't I need an account?

Your single-use purchase code is your receipt. Paste it in, the credits land in your browser's wallet, and that's it. Fewer accounts, fewer passwords, less of your data floating around.

Can I add text to my image?

Yes — step 4 is a full text studio: add as many text lines as you want, each one an independent layer with its own font (29 popular Google Fonts), size, color, position, rotation and style. Edit fullscreen, drag text right on the image, and it's burned into the final download. Adding text alone is free; it only uses a credit when you also run the AI extension.
